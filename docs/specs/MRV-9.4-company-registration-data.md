# MRV-9.4 Account type (business or personal) and registration data: CNPJ, CPF and related

**Goal.** At registration a customer chooses a **business account** or a **personal account**; the site then collects and validates the right Brazilian registration data for each, so staff can quote, invoice, deliver and (for alcohol) sell lawfully.

**Owner input (2026-10-30).** About 90% of sales go to businesses but some go to people; both must be able to open an account and each must be handled according to their data. (This replaces the earlier "B2B only, no CPF" assumption.)

**What the Brazilian identifiers mean (Captain's research from the Receita Federal rules, from knowledge and not fetched; the Worker must confirm the check-digit rules and the alphanumeric format against the official Receita note before building).**
- **CNPJ** (company/branch tax number): 14 positions = 8 company root (**raiz**) + 4 establishment order (`0001` head office = **matriz**, `0002+` = **filiais**) + 2 check digits (módulo 11). Since July 2026 the Receita issues **alphanumeric CNPJs** (letters and digits in the first 12 positions, numeric check digits); numeric ones stay valid. The calculation uses each character's ASCII code minus 48.
- **CPF** (individual tax number): 11 digits = 9 base + 2 check digits (módulo 11); sequences of one repeated digit are invalid. It is personal data under LGPD.
- Business data normally asked of a wholesale buyer: **razão social**, **nome fantasia**, **inscrição estadual (IE)** (ICMS registration needed for the NF-e and ICMS-ST) or the status **isento** / **não contribuinte**, **inscrição municipal**, **CNAE principal**, **regime tributário** (MEI, Simples Nacional, Lucro Presumido, Lucro Real), **data de abertura**, **e-mail para NF-e**, **telefone**, **responsável**.
- Selling alcohol to anyone under 18 is prohibited in Brazil, and this is a beverage distributor: a **personal account therefore declares a date of birth and must be 18 or older**.

**Decisions (Captain, reversible).**
- `ClientAccount.AccountType` is `Business` or `Personal`, chosen at registration with a clear two-option choice (default none selected; the form shows the matching fields after the choice). Business accounts use the **company name** as the display name; personal accounts use the **full name**. The existing `CompanyName` column keeps holding the display name (the label changes with the type), so nothing downstream breaks.
- Registration asks only for the essentials of each type (business: display name, CNPJ optional; personal: full name, CPF, date of birth); everything else is completed on the profile. A Pending customer can change the type; after approval only an admin can convert it (audited), because documents were issued under the old identity.
- Business and personal fields are optional where they are not legally needed to register, **except** the personal account's CPF and date of birth (needed for the age rule and for the nota fiscal), which are required.
- **Owner rule (2026-10-30): once approved, the verified registration record cannot be changed by the customer; only an admin has full control.** The record is verified at approval, and changing it later would be legally complicated. Locked for the customer after approval: account type, display/company name, `Cnpj`, `Cpf`, `LegalName`, `TradeName`, `StateRegistration*`, `MunicipalRegistration`, `MainCnae`, `TaxRegime`, `OpeningDate`, `BirthDate`. Still editable by the customer: addresses (MRV-9.3, delivery needs change), phone, invoice e-mail, contact person and password. Every admin change is audited with before/after (CPF masked). The profile shows locked fields as read-only with the reason.
- **CPF handling (LGPD minimisation):** stored encrypted at rest (ASP.NET Data Protection) with a keyed hash column for uniqueness; shown in full only to the owner and the admin, **masked** (`***.456.789-**`) in the staff widget and page, in the audit log and in logs; never returned by any public endpoint. CNPJ is public registry data but is shown only to the customer, staff and admin.
- No call to a government or third-party registry in this story (needs a server-side client, key policy and privacy review): follow-up for auto-fill and situação cadastral.
- IE is checked by length (2–14) and characters only (27 state algorithms); CNAE by format (7 digits). A CNPJ or CPF already used by another account answers 409 without revealing whose.
- An incomplete registration record does **not** block approval; staff see a completeness indicator. (The owner may tighten this later.)

## Acceptance criteria
- AC-1 New columns on `ClientAccount` (or an owned `ClientRegistration` type), with a migration and `Down`: `AccountType` (non-null; existing rows become `Business` in the dev database only (the owner categorises real customers when the production database is migrated: the migration notes in `docs/tests` give the SQL/admin steps, and the demo seed gains one personal account)), common `Phone` (10–11 digits, ≤30 as typed), `InvoiceEmail` (valid, ≤200); Business: `Cnpj` (14 chars normalised, unique when present), `LegalName` (≤200), `TradeName` (≤200), `StateRegistrationStatus` (`Contributor` | `Exempt` | `NonContributor`), `StateRegistration` (required when `Contributor`, forbidden otherwise), `MunicipalRegistration` (≤30), `MainCnae` (7 digits), `TaxRegime`, `OpeningDate` (not in the future), `ContactName` (≤120); Personal: `Cpf` (encrypted, with hash for uniqueness), `BirthDate` (age ≥ 18 on the day, not before 1900). Fields of the other type must be empty (400).
- AC-2 `CnpjRules` and `CpfRules` (domain, one place each) normalise (strip `. / - ` and spaces, uppercase) and validate: CNPJ 14 chars, first 12 in `[0-9A-Z]`, last 2 numeric, not a repeated digit, módulo-11 digits with weights `5 4 3 2 9 8 7 6 5 4 3 2` and `6 5 4 3 2 9 8 7 6 5 4 3 2`; CPF 11 digits, not a repeated digit, módulo-11 digits. Unit tests: known-valid numeric CNPJs, a valid alphanumeric CNPJ from the Receita's published examples, a wrong check digit, a letter in a check position, repeated digits, wrong lengths, valid and invalid CPFs, a birth date that is exactly 18 today (valid) and one day short (invalid).
- AC-3 Registration and `PUT /api/client/profile` accept the type-specific fields; invalid values answer 400 naming the field; duplicates answer 409 "CNPJ/CPF já cadastrado" without revealing whose; an under-18 birth date answers 400 with a pt-BR message explaining the age rule.
- AC-4 After approval a customer's attempt to change any locked field answers 403 telling them to contact the company; Pending customers and admins may change them; Suspended customers cannot edit. Admin conversion between types clears the other type's fields only after confirmation and is audited.
- AC-5 Registration page: first a labelled two-option choice ("Conta empresarial" / "Conta pessoal") with a one-line explanation each; then the matching fields (business: nome da empresa, CNPJ optional with masked input accepting letters; personal: nome completo, CPF masked, data de nascimento with the 18+ message), then e-mail, password, the billing address (MRV-9.3) and the privacy link. Server and client validation messages are pt-BR; the form aligns on shared edges and passes the layout audit and axe at all widths.
- AC-6 Profile page: a "Dados cadastrais" section whose fields depend on the account type, in the order CNPJ (with derived read-only "Matriz"/"Filial" from the order digits), razão social, nome fantasia, IE status and IE (enabled only for Contribuinte), inscrição municipal, CNAE, regime tributário, data de abertura, e-mail para NF-e, telefone, responsável; or for personal accounts CPF, data de nascimento (read-only after approval), telefone, e-mail para nota. Locked fields explain why.
- AC-7 Staff views (MRV-9.2 widget and page, admin customers) show the account type badge ("Empresa"/"Pessoa física"), the registration data formatted (CNPJ masked, CPF **masked**), the completeness indicator and, for admins, an edit panel; admin sees the full CPF only on explicit "Mostrar CPF" action, which writes an audit entry.
- AC-8 Privacy page lists the new data (including CPF and date of birth) under "Dados coletados" with purposes (cadastro, verificação de maioridade para venda de bebidas alcoólicas, emissão de documentos fiscais, entrega) and a bumped last-updated date, still marked as a draft for legal review. Registration data is never returned by public endpoints or the contact flow.
- AC-9 API and client tests cover: each validation, uniqueness, the lock-after-approval rule, the age rule, role access (a customer sees only their own; 401/403), CPF masking in staff responses and audit entries, encryption at rest (the stored column is not the plain CPF), the masks, the IE toggle and the type switch at registration. Coverage map, `project.md`, `architecture.md`, `CONTEXT.md` (**Account type**, **CNPJ**, **CPF**, **State registration**) updated; suites pass three times; layout audit and axe stay at zero.

## Out of scope
Registry lookup or auto-fill, per-state IE validation, document upload (contrato social, comprovante, identity document), ID-document age verification (declaration only in this story: the delivery driver still checks), credit checks, linking several CNPJs under one account, e-invoicing integration, a customer deleting their account (separate LGPD story).
